Everything a club needs

One game, one browser tab, no download required.

Player market

Over a thousand invented players in fifty clubs. Buy at the asking price or haggle for a discount.

Academy scouting

Scout up to twenty youngsters a season. A scout can never beat your own team overall, so you have to earn the big finds.

Coaches

Ninety-seven coaches to choose from. Each one brings a formation and a rating boost.

Wages and sponsors

Stars are expensive. Balance a growing wage bill against sponsor money and prize cash.

Injuries and bans

Players get hurt, collect cards and miss matches. Your bench matters.

Crests and photos

Upload your club crest and player photos. In the online league everyone sees them.

Packs with a guarantee

Six pack types, from everyday packs up to the Epic pack. Buy ten at once and a top-rated player is guaranteed, so a big spend never ends empty.

A league that remembers

Play a season

Every club plays every other. Wins and draws pay bonuses, and the top three take the prize money.

Go up, or down

Finish high and move up a division. Finish low and you drop. Two tiers keep every match meaningful.

Play online

Join the shared league with friends. Clubs, crests and photos stay in sync between devices.

How to play

1
Pick your club

Open the game and choose a club, or make your own with a name and crest.

2
Set your lineup

Choose your starting XI and formation. Players in the right position play better.

3
Play your matches

Every match earns money. Wins and draws pay more.

4
Grow the club

Open packs, sign players, sell extras and upgrade your stadium, academy and training ground.

5
Climb the table

Finish high to go up a division. Share your club link so friends can join online.
